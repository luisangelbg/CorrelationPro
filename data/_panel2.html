¿Qué correlación uso?Which correlation should I use?

Elige dos columnas: el asistente mira su tipo y, si son continuas, la forma de su nube (atípicos, curvatura, empates, si sube y baja), y propone las medidas en orden, con la razón de cada una.Pick two columns: the assistant looks at their kind and, if they are continuous, the shape of their cloud (outliers, curvature, ties, whether it rises and falls), and proposes the measures in order, with the reason for each.
